Speak with WeType.

READY TO RUN

One small install.
One less manual switch.

Have WeType installed and its push-to-talk key configured. Choose either installer.

Prebuilt app · No developer tools needed
Installation commands ↗
CURL
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zl190/hijack/main/install.sh | sh
OR, WITH HOMEBREW
brew install --cask zl190/tap/hijack

After installation, allow Hijack in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Click its menu bar icon to choose your trigger key. It follows WeType’s voice key by default.

View releases ↗
Who processes my voice?

WeType handles speech recognition. Hijack switches input sources and forwards trigger-key events; it does not record, store, or upload audio. Consult WeType’s privacy policy for audio processing, network transfer, and retention.
