## Hijack 1.1.0

- **Any voice tool**: WeType, Doubao, Sogou and Handy work out of the box. Hijack reads each tool's own voice key when it can, and you can override it.
- **Hands-free mode**: tap to start, tap again (or press any key) to stop. Hold-to-talk still works.
- **Settings window**: Dictation, Sources, General and Advanced tabs, with a key recorder that knows left and right modifiers. The menu keeps the everyday choices.
- **Config file**: `~/.config/hijack/config.json` is the source of truth; ⌘, opens it. A file that doesn't parse is never overwritten.
- **Command line**: `hijack status`, `doctor`, `sources`, `get`, `set` and `log`, with `--json` for scripts and agents.
- **New icon**: a layered macOS 26 icon with light, dark, clear and tinted appearances.
- Chinese and English interface. Fixes for long dictations losing text and for missed or doubled key presses.
